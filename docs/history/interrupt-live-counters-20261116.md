记录：overlay-163057-896580；日志20261116-163100-698036-STC32G144K246_Simulation_Test。已恢复全部2560字节。候选HEX SHA256：d230e9e104447f6dd5200a1d1bb2f6ac1a4dac2ab32529c00dc8654ff62309b5。
